Our project mainly uses AI as a brainstorm instrument. In our beginning stage we use AI to help us clarify division of labor for each person and determine everyone’s work aim and summary. After that we use AI to generate a poll for our teammates to choose their expected work. Definitely, this work was after our discussion, so AI was just utilized to clarify. The subsequent writing of content, code, visualizations and PPT creation were all done manually. Worth to remind, some content writing was helped by AI polishing, which means asking AI for if there exists any grammatical errors or improper words and then manually correcting them, not meaning copying or plagiarizing. In these two parts, we mainly use chatGPT.
